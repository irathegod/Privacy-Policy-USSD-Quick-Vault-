Privacy Policy for USSD Quick Vault Last Updated: October 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how **USSD Quick Vault** ("the App," "we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting user privacy, ensuring transparency regarding system permissions, and maintaining the security of all locally stored data. 1. Core Purpose of the App USSD Quick Vault is an assistive utility designed to help users interact efficiently with carrier USSD sessions. It assists individuals—particularly those with motor challenges, cognitive load issues, or repetitive data-entry requirements—by automating the input of frequently used credentials (such as account numbers, meter IDs and menu selections) and selecting contacts directly within carrier USSD dialog menus. 2. Prominent Disclosure: Use of Android AccessibilityService API To fulfill its core assistive functionality, USSD Quick Vault requires the activation of Android’s AccessibilityService API. * Why Existing APIs Cannot Be Used: The standard Android Autofill Framework does not operate within system telephony dialogs or carrier USSD prompt overlays (`android.widget.EditText` inside carrier dialer overlays). The Accessibility API is the only Android system framework capable of detecting active carrier USSD text nodes and programmatically inserting text using `AccessibilityNodeInfo.ACTION_SET_TEXT`. * Scope of Use: The AccessibilityService is utilized strictly when an active USSD session is detected on your screen to identify target input fields and populate your chosen saved snippet or credential upon your command. * Strict Privacy Safeguards: * The AccessibilityService never monitors, reads, captures, or logs background screen content, personal messages, keystrokes outside USSD menus or sensitive credentials. * The AccessibilityService does NOT transmit any screen information or user interactions over the network. * You can enable or disable this permission at any time via your device’s "Settings > Accessibility > USSD Quick Vault". 3. Data Handling, Local Processing & Storage * On-Device Storage: All user-created snippets, account identifiers, meter numbers, phone numbers, and configuration settings are processed entirely on-device. * Encryption: All saved data is stored locally within encrypted private application storage (utilizing Android’s internal private app sandbox and cryptographic storage standards). * No Server Storage: We do not operate remote databases or external servers that store, process, or maintain copies of your private credentials or USSD interaction histories. * No Third-Party Sharing: Your personal credentials and input data are never sold, rented, shared, or exposed to third parties. 4. Internet Access & Anonymous Telemetry The application declares the `` permission in its manifest solely for application maintenance: * Telemetry & Stability: The App may transmit anonymous crash logs, technical performance diagnostics and stability telemetry (such as device model, OS version, and crash stack traces). * Strict Non-PII Guarantee: This diagnostic telemetry contains **no personally identifiable information (PII)**, no saved snippets, no phone numbers and no USSD content. * Purpose Limitation: Internet access is restricted exclusively to monitoring technical health and resolving software bugs to ensure the application remains stable. 5. Data Retention & Deletion Because all user data is stored locally on your device: * You can modify or delete individual snippets directly from the App’s interface at any time. * You can permanently erase all stored credentials and application configurations by clearing the App's data via "Settings > Apps > USSD Quick Vault > Storage > Clear Data", or by uninstalling the App from your device. 6. Children's Privacy USSD Quick Vault does not address or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. 7.
